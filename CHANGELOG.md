# Changelog

Changes to the RDstat web app that can affect results are marked **(results)**. The version is shown in the app's About tab and in every downloaded file.

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)
- "R code to repeat this analysis" card at the end of each results screen (t-test, correlation, regression, analysis of variance including the split plot and the analysis combined over sites) with a Copy button. The script uses base R and agricolae only, carries the example data (or reads your file with its separator and the same number parsing as the app) and gives the same numbers as the screen. Not available for an RCBD with missing responses.
- The app code is split into `prototype/app/src/` (helpers, screens, one server file per screen); behaviour is unchanged. Notes on the problems met are in `docs/DEV_NOTES.md`.
- First public build: ANOVA (CRD, RCBD, split plot), correlation and linear regression.
- Uploaded files: Thai CSV files saved by Excel on Windows (Windows-874 / TIS-620) and Excel's "Unicode Text" (UTF-16) files are read correctly; title lines above the column names and empty columns are skipped, and the app says what it skipped.
- **(results)** Summary rows typed below the data (Mean, Total, CV (%), LSD 0.05, SE, เฉลี่ย, รวม, ...) are no longer read as plots; earlier builds analysed such a row as one more treatment.
- **(results)** Labels typed once for several rows are repeated on every row: cells merged down a column in Excel (e.g. a variety name merged over its replications), and in CSV files a label column left blank under each label in groups of equal size. Earlier builds dropped the rows with a blank label, so most of the plots were left out of the analysis. A heading of two rows with merged cells now gives the column names of the second row.
- For an uploaded file, the first choices of block, environment and treatment on the ANOVA screen come from the column names (Rep, Block, ซ้ำ, บล็อก; Site, Season, Year, สถานที่, ฤดู) instead of the column order, so a file laid out Variety, Rep, Yield no longer starts with Variety as the block. Another measured trait is not offered first as a treatment.
- **(results)** Uploaded files: a note typed beside one plot (in an extra column without a name) no longer moves the header down to a data row, which dropped the column names and the plots above the note; the notes column is left out and listed. A title merged over a block of cells in Excel (several rows and columns) is no longer copied into every cell, which made the upload fail.
- A column named B (factor B next to a factor A) is no longer guessed as the block; a column named Rep or Block is preferred.
- Rank-based tests can be chosen as the analysis: on the Compare treatments screen (formerly Analysis of variance), Analysis = rank-based gives the Kruskal-Wallis (CRD) or Friedman (RCBD) test with medians, letters, a box plot, an APA sentence, a Thai summary, Excel and Word files and R code in place of the ANOVA; on the Two groups screen (formerly t-test), Test = Wilcoxon does the same in place of the t-test. The descriptive statistics of the two-group screen now include the median.
- After running, the results screen checks the data and recommends a method, with a button to switch and one to keep the current method (advice only): residual normality with a mixed rule (30 or more per group with moderate skewness and kurtosis counts as robust; fewer than 15 gets a low-power warning), outliers before a rank test, then equal variances (Levene). Unequal variances suggest Welch's ANOVA in a CRD and a transformation or the Friedman test in an RCBD. Only the chosen method's results are shown (the rank-based test is no longer shown beside the ANOVA and the t-test), so that the method is not picked by its p value. The method buttons now switch the results at once, with no new run.
- **(results)** The standard ANOVA (and the pooled t-test for two groups) stays the default, as analysts expect. After the run the results screen checks the assumptions and, when the variances differ (or the group sizes are unequal and the variances differ by a factor of 2 or more), recommends Welch's ANOVA (Games-Howell comparisons, CRD with one factor) or Welch's t-test, with a button to switch (Delacre et al. 2017, 2019). Analyses that do not exist for the chosen design (Welch and trimmed means for blocks, rank-based and the others for two factors, split plots and combined analyses) are greyed out in the Analysis buttons, with the reason.
- The assumption checks are shown as a traffic light, in the advice box and on the Outliers & assumptions tab: green (met), yellow (a mild departure the analysis is robust to: large samples with a moderate shape, moderately unequal variances with equal group sizes, suspect values, or too few observations for the tests to have power) and red (a clear violation or outliers: another method is advised). On red the advice offers a method for each reporting goal: ranks or medians (Kruskal-Wallis, Friedman, Wilcoxon), or means without relying on the normal curve (trimmed means).
- Trimmed means (20%) with percentile bootstrap intervals, for a CRD with one factor (heteroscedastic ANOVA on trimmed means with Yuen's tests, Holm adjusted, for the letters) and for two groups (Yuen's test; the trimmed mean of the differences for paired data).
- Welch's ANOVA with Games-Howell comparisons for a CRD with one factor, and Dunn's test (Holm or Bonferroni adjusted) as an option after the Kruskal-Wallis test; Conover's comparison stays the default.
- Only the engine files the app uses are bundled, so the app opens faster.
- About tab with license, citation and problem-reporting link; app version written to Excel and TXT downloads.
- Home page ("What do you want to do?"), Methods page with a copyable methods sentence, collapsible "What does this mean?" help, maintainer and disclaimer in About, app name kept in one place (`app_name`), deployment notes for IT in `docs/DEPLOY.md`.
- Excel files are written with writexl and read with readxl (instead of openxlsx and its large dependency stringi): about 12 MB less to download when the app opens.
- Mean comparison (LSD, Tukey, Duncan, SNK, Scheffe, Bonferroni) now uses the agricolae package; the normality test uses nortest and the Breusch-Pagan test uses lmtest (identical numbers to the old code). The Methods page builds the reference list from each package's citation.
- The mean comparison choices in the app are limited to LSD, Tukey HSD and Duncan (DMRT), plus Automatic (SNK, Scheffe and Bonferroni remain available in the R engine).
- **(results)** Scheffe letters were wrong in earlier builds: the alpha chosen in the app was ignored (always 0.05) and the letters were as for Tukey's test. Fixed in the tables (via agricolae) and in the classic STAR report (the recovered code used F instead of sqrt((k-1)F) as the critical value). Re-run any Scheffe comparison made before this version.
- **(results)** With unbalanced data, the N column shows each group's real count instead of the harmonic mean; Tukey uses the Tukey-Kramer method when group sizes differ.
- Methods page now explains what each analysis does (models for CRD, RCBD and split plot, means comparison, assumption checks, correlation, regression) with the matching references.
- Text is set in the Chulabhorn Likhit font (Chulabhorn Royal Academy); the font files are in prototype/app/www/fonts.
- App renamed RDstat (Rice Department); downloaded files are named `rdstat_*`. Home, Methods and About pages are in Thai (the copyable methods sentence and references stay in English).
- Plot style: colours (Okabe-Ito, greyscale, Viridis, Set2, greens), theme, font size and legend position, shared by every plot; downloaded plots can be sized for a journal column, a slide or a custom size in cm.
- t-test screen: two independent groups (pooled and Welch, as in STAR, with the F test for equal variances; with more than two groups the user picks two), paired, and one sample; descriptive statistics, confidence interval of the difference, Cohen's d, Shapiro-Wilk checks, plot of the observations with means and CIs, Excel download.
- Split plot in CRD added to Analysis of variance (main plots assigned at random, no blocks; Error(a) = replications within the main-plot factor). Results match STAR's SplitCRD analysis.
- t-test comes before Analysis of variance in the menu and on Home (Home cards in a 2 x 2 grid); the numbered sidebar steps are separate cards with clearer headings.
- Laptop screens: on wide windows the sidebar has a fixed width (280-360 px) so the tables get the rest, and wide tables scroll sideways in their own box; below 992 px the sidebar sits above the results. Numbers in tables use tabular figures (Chulabhorn Likhit has none) so the decimal points of a column line up.
- Fewer misreadings: the upload box shows the long (one row per plot) layout the analyses need, and a wide-format file (columns Rep 1, Rep 2 ... or ซ้ำที่ 1, ซ้ำที่ 2 ...) is recognised and can be converted with one click. A means table without letters now says why (F test not significant, with its p value, or a significant interaction). Split plot: clearer labels for the block / main-plot number column and a live check of the layout that shows the Error(a) df the choice gives and catches a main-plot or sub-plot number chosen by mistake. When the settings change after a run, the old results are greyed out and cannot be copied or downloaded until the analysis is run again ("Run again" button in the warning).
- Blank Excel templates: download ready-to-fill templates directly in the upload panel tailored to the selected design (CRD, RCBD, SplitCRD, SplitRCBD).
- Guardrails for ANOVA variables: Response variable is restricted to numeric columns (`Response variable (numeric)`) with automatic exclusion from treatment factors.
- Means in publication layout (GenStat / ARM style): each factor's means table ends with the F test (** p < 0.01, * p < 0.05, ns), Grand mean, CV (%), SE(m), SED and LSD from the error mean square that tests that factor (Error(a) / Error(b) in a split plot). It replaces the separate Agronomy summary card; one-click copy, and a "Publication table" sheet in the Excel file (the Means sheet also gets SE(m), SED and LSD columns).
- Formal diagnostic summary cards: clean academic cards summarizing residual normality (Shapiro-Wilk), variance equality (Levene), and outlier counts with formal Bootstrap badges (no informal icons/emojis).
- Data transformation in ANOVA: optional log(y + 1), sqrt(y + 0.5) or arcsine of sqrt(y / 100) in degrees (Gomez & Gomez, chapter 7). Means are also shown back-transformed to the original unit; the transformation is documented in the results, the Excel Info sheet and the Methods page. The arcsine refuses values outside 0-100 instead of silently capping them.
- **(results)** The Means sheet of the ANOVA Excel file no longer carries letters that the screen hides (effects without a significant F test, or main effects when the interaction is significant).
- Fixed (in this unreleased work): the Excel download failed for every two-factor and split-plot ANOVA (the publication sheet could not be assembled), and the means plot failed when a transformation was chosen with "Show observations" on (the observations were not transformed).
- Diagnostic cards: "Not tested" no longer comes with the text "Residuals depart from normality", and an untestable variance check in an ANOVA no longer mentions regression.
- Combined analysis over sites / seasons / years (RCBD or CRD, one treatment factor) in Analysis of variance, as STAR's combAOVTest: environment tested against replications within environment (RCBD), treatment and treatment x environment against the pooled error, simple effects when the interaction is significant, the analysis of each environment and Bartlett's test of their error variances. Results match combAOVTest on the Gomez & Gomez two-season rice data, which is the built-in example (plus a simulated CRD pot trial at three sites). Blank Excel templates for both.
- Rank-based (nonparametric) alternatives: Kruskal-Wallis (CRD) and Friedman (RCBD) for one-factor ANOVA, with letters on ranks (agricolae), in the Outliers & assumptions tab (the Results tab leaves the assumption checks to the analyst); Wilcoxon rank-sum (Mann-Whitney) and signed-rank tests with the Hodges-Lehmann estimate and interval on the t-test screen and in its Excel file.
- Summary in Thai on the ANOVA and t-test results (copyable), next to the English one.
- Download report (Word): one .doc file (single-file web page, opens in Microsoft Word) with the settings, Thai and English summaries, ANOVA and publication tables, assumption checks, rank-based test, the plot, the methods sentence and the references. Needs no extra R package, so it also works in the browser.
- The app style sheet is longer than the 8192 characters sprintf() accepts, so the brand colours are now filled in by css_fill().
- Live View for main plots: figures intended for reports and publications (ANOVA, t-test, Correlation, Regression) are framed inside a dedicated preview card with proportional aspect ratio matching the chosen export size (Standard 20x14 cm, Journal 1-column, Journal 2-column, Slide 16:9, or Custom), with controls to toggle title, subtitle/notes, and X-axis label angles (0°, 45°, 90°). The Outliers & assumptions tab remains full-width and unconstrained for diagnostic inspection.
- User feedback & error reporting integration: top navbar "ส่งข้อเสนอแนะ" link, About tab feedback action, and direct "แจ้งปัญหานี้" buttons on error alerts across ANOVA, t-test, Correlation, and Regression screens. Clicking opens a feedback modal linking directly to a Google Form, paired with an automated system diagnostic summary (app/R version, OS, timestamp, active screen parameters, and exact error details) with one-click clipboard copying for non-technical researchers.
